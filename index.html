Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about tummy tuck surgery, recovery, and candidacy at our Hoboken practice.

1. What is the difference between a full abdominoplasty surgery and a mini tummy tuck procedure?

A full abdominoplasty surgery addresses excess skin and separated muscles across the entire abdominal wall—both above and below the belly button—and repositions the navel for balanced abdominal reshaping surgery. A mini tummy tuck procedure focuses exclusively on tightening loose skin and mild laxity below the navel using a smaller incision.

2. Who is an ideal candidate for post pregnancy tummy tuck or post weight loss tummy tuck surgery?

Ideal candidates are healthy, non-smoking adults at a stable weight who experience loose abdominal skin, stubborn tissue folds, or core muscle separation (diastasis recti) that will not respond to diet and exercise alone. During your consultation with our plastic surgeon, we determine whether a standard, mini, or extended tummy tuck surgery best fits your anatomy.

3. Does cosmetic tummy tuck surgery include abdominal muscle repair?

Yes. Most full and extended tummy tuck NJ procedures incorporate internal abdominal muscle repair to suture stretched or separated rectus muscles back together. This restores core stability, narrows the waistline, and provides the structural foundation needed for effective flat stomach surgery.

4. How long is recovery after abdominal contouring surgery, and when will I see final results?

Most patients return to desk work and light daily routines within 2 to 3 weeks, while strenuous exercise and heavy lifting are gradually resumed after 6 weeks. Swelling steadily subsides over the first few months, revealing your smooth, natural looking tummy tuck contours between 3 and 6 months post-surgery.

5. Where will the scar be located after excess skin removal surgery?

Our plastic surgeon places the incision low along the natural bikini line so it remains easily concealed beneath underwear and swimwear. With our advanced tummy tuck technique and proper post-operative scar care, the incision line fades significantly over time while delivering transformative body contouring procedure results.
